StillMind is a private voice journal designed to help you slow down, capture what is on your mind, and notice the patterns that matter.

Record a short reflection or write one instead. StillMind transcribes your voice, creates a concise summary, and organises useful details such as themes, moods, and action items—all on your device.

CAPTURE THOUGHTS NATURALLY
- Record a voice reflection or write a text entry
- Review and edit transcripts before saving
- Turn longer thoughts into clear, useful summaries

REFLECT WITHOUT JUDGMENT
- Notice gentle mood patterns over time
- See your journaling rhythm at a glance
- Revisit themes, people, and next steps

STAY ORGANISED
- Group related entries into projects
- Search transcripts, summaries, and tags
- Export entries as clean PDFs

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Journal content stays on your device
- Audio and entry data are encrypted at rest
- Encryption keys are protected in the Keychain
- Face ID can keep your journal locked
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics, subscriptions, or in-app purchases

BACK UP ON YOUR TERMS
- Create password-protected encrypted backups
- Restore your journal on another device
- Configure automatic local backups

StillMind is free to use. Your thoughts are yours.
